# StackEZ Instagram Carousel: Budget 2026 - 100,000 Workers Trained in AI


SLIDE 1
Headline: 100,000 Workers. AI-Ready by 2029.
Subtext: Singapore just launched a national programme to bring AI into every industry. Here's what it means for your business.


SLIDE 2
Headline: A new national programme to make workers AI-fluent.
Key points:
* The National AI Impact Programme (NAIIP) was announced by Minister Josephine Teo on 2 March 2026.
* The target: train 100,000 workers to apply AI in their jobs by 2029.
* It also aims to support 10,000 enterprises in adopting AI over the next three years.
* The programme builds on Singapore's National AI Strategy 2.0, led by IMDA.
Why it matters: The government is making a clear push. AI is no longer optional. It is becoming part of how every industry works.


SLIDE 3
Headline: AI training is starting with legal and accountancy, and expanding fast.
Key points:
* Accountants will learn to use AI for financial reporting, compliance monitoring and automating manual tasks.
* Lawyers will be trained to use AI for legal research, document review and contract management, freeing them to focus on strategy and client work.
* Software engineers will be upskilled to become full-stack developers who can manage complex systems and workflows powered by AI agents.
* IMDA is partnering with bodies like the Institute of Singapore Chartered Accountants, Singapore Academy of Law and Singapore Corporate Counsel Association.
* More sectors, including HR, are next in line. Programmes are set to begin in the first half of 2026.
Why it matters: You do not need to be in the tech industry to benefit. AI fluency is being built into everyday professional roles across sectors.


SLIDE 4
Headline: SMEs get pre-approved AI solutions and grant support.
Key points:
* Currently, 15% of SMEs and about 7 in 10 workers already use AI in some way.
* IMDA and Enterprise Singapore have curated and pre-approved market-proven, cost-effective AI solutions from reputable vendors.
* Grant support is available to reduce the cost of adoption for businesses.
* The TechSkills Accelerator (TeSA) programme, which has upskilled over 440,000 individuals since 2016, is now expanding into non-tech sectors.
Why it matters: As Minister Teo put it, "We want to encourage those who haven't started to take the first step." Small businesses stand to gain the most from adopting AI, and the support is there to help you get started.


SLIDE 5
Headline: Free AI tools, better learning paths and more access for workers.
Key points:
* SkillsFuture is being redesigned to make AI learning pathways clearer and easier to navigate.
* Workers who sign up for selected AI courses will get 6 months of free access to premium AI tools to practise what they learn.
* AI literacy will be strengthened across all institutes of higher learning (IHLs) to prepare future workers.
* SkillsFuture and Workforce Singapore will be merged into a single agency under MOE and MOM, creating a one-stop shop for skills training, career guidance and job matching.
Why it matters: The resources and tools are being made available. Whether you are an employee looking to upskill or a business owner planning ahead, there is a clear path now.
