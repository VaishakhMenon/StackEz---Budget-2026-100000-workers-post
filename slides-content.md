SLIDE 1 (Cover Slide)
Headline: Singapore is Training **100,000 Workers** to Use **AI** by 2029.
Subtext: Announced at the Committee of Supply debate on 2 Mar 2026 by Minister Josephine Teo.


SLIDE 2 — [1] The National AI Impact Programme is here.

* **100,000 workers** will be trained to apply AI in their jobs by 2029.

* **10,000 enterprises** will be supported in AI adoption over three years.

* It builds on Singapore's **National AI Strategy 2.0**, supported by MOM and MOE.

* The vision: make workers **"bilingual"** in both their profession and AI.

**This is not just for tech companies. It is for every industry.**


SLIDE 3 — [2] AI training starts with accountancy and legal.

* **Accountants** will use AI for financial reporting and compliance monitoring.

* **Lawyers** will use AI for research, document review and contract management.

* **Software engineers** will be upskilled to manage workflows powered by AI agents.

* IMDA is partnering with **ISCA, SAL and SCCA** to develop these programmes.

**Programmes launch in the first half of 2026.**


SLIDE 4 — [3] SMEs get pre-approved AI tools and grant support.

* Only **15% of SMEs** use AI today — but **7 in 10 workers** already do.

* **IMDA and Enterprise Singapore** have pre-approved cost-effective AI solutions from reputable vendors.

* **Grant support** is available to reduce the cost of adoption.

**"Small businesses stand to gain the most from adopting AI."** — Minister Josephine Teo


SLIDE 5 — [4] TeSA is expanding beyond tech — for the first time.

* TeSA has upskilled over **440,000 individuals** and placed **24,300 locals** in tech roles since 2016.

* It is now expanding into **non-tech sectors** for the first time — starting with accountancy and legal.

* Workers will get **hands-on AI training** and learn responsible AI practices.

**AI training is no longer just for tech workers.**
